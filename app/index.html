Nielsen–Thurston type

log λ is the braid's dilatation: any motion realising the braid stretches its surroundings at least by λ per period. The braid is read from the world-lines projected on an axis; its conjugacy class (hence λ, type, permutation, exponent sum) does not depend on the axis.

Free fall orbits retrace themselves, so their full-period braid is trivial (ββ⁻¹ = 1); the census uses the half-period braid closed by a straight-line return. Lyapunov exponents are from Hristov et al.
